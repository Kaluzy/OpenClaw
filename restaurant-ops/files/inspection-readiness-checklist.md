# Health Inspection Readiness Checklist

## Daily
- [ ] Handwashing sinks stocked (soap, towels, signage)
- [ ] Cold holding <= 41°F
- [ ] Hot holding >= 135°F
- [ ] Food date labels complete
- [ ] Sanitizer buckets at correct concentration
- [ ] Dish area clean and sanitizer test strips available
- [ ] Floors and drains clean/dry
- [ ] Trash covered and removed on schedule
- [ ] Pest evidence check complete
- [ ] Employee illness policy posted and acknowledged

## Weekly
- [ ] Deep-clean line equipment and gaskets
- [ ] Walk-in organization and FIFO audit
- [ ] Thermometer calibration check
- [ ] Chemical storage separated/labeled
- [ ] Back-of-house wall/ceiling wipe-down

## Monthly
- [ ] Full mock inspection walkthrough
- [ ] Review corrective action log trends
- [ ] Retrain staff on top 3 recurring misses
- [ ] Replace worn labels/signage/supplies
