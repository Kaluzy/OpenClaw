Our promise

If something is sponsored, we label it clearly. Sponsored placements help fund the project — but we’ll keep it parent-first.

How we label

Sponsored
A paid placement (spotlight, category sponsor, or directory boost).
Partner
A business we have a relationship with (may include discounts or affiliate-style referrals).
Editorial pick
Chosen because it’s genuinely useful. Not paid.
