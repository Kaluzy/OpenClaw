Questions

Straight answers

Is this a free audit?

No. This is a done-for-you install. I may flag a couple of issues as part of the quote, but the deliverable is implementation and a before/after summary, not a report.

Do you need my passwords?

No. For Google, you add me as a manager through their standard interface. For your website, you can usually add an editor account. If neither is possible, Lane B avoids it entirely.

How fast will I see results?

Some things are immediate: your page starts converting better, review asks go out after every job, and your Google listing looks complete. Google map rankings can take a few weeks to reflect changes - that's why we document a baseline and track it.

What if I'm not local?

Fill out the form anyway. If it's a fit, I'll say so. If not, I'll tell you quickly. (I keep the install narrow so I can deliver fast and clean.)

Is there a monthly fee after?

No. This is a fixed one-time install. If you want ongoing work after the 7 days, we can discuss it, but there is no recurring charge built into this.
